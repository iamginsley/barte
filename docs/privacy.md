# Privacy Policy for Barte

[Deutsche Version](datenschutz)

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Barte is a free iOS app for sharing expenses with friends. This policy describes which data that involves.

## In short

- You sign in with **“Sign in with Apple”**. Barte never gets a password.
- Your groups are stored on a server run by **Nhost in the EU (Frankfurt)**, so everyone in a group sees the same expenses.
- **No ads, no tracking, no analytics.** Data is not sold and not shared with third parties.
- You can delete your account in the app at any time.

## Data the app processes

- **Account:** a random user ID and the email address Apple provides when you sign in. If you choose “Hide My Email”, this is an anonymous relay address from Apple. The email address is only used for signing in; Barte doesn't send you emails.
- **Groups:** name, color, currency and an invite code
- **Your name:** your account name (your first name from Apple when you sign in, changeable under “About Barte”). It appears in every group you're in
- **People:** the names you enter in a group for people who don't use Barte, and, for people who use Barte, the link to their user ID
- **Expenses:** title, amount, day, who paid, for whom and how it is split
- **History:** who added, changed or deleted an expense in a group and when, with the values before and after, plus renames and closing a group. The history protects everyone in the group from unnoticed changes, so it can't be edited and is only deleted together with the group
- **Payment details (optional):** your PayPal.me name, and your IBAN and account holder, if you enter them under “Payment Details”. Barte shows them when settling up so others can pay you directly. Barte itself doesn't process payments; tapping “PayPal” opens paypal.me with the amount.
- **Chat:** messages you write in a group, with their time and, if any, the expense they refer to, plus how far you've read the chat and whether you muted it. Only the people in the group see messages. You can delete your own messages at any time; only the note “Message deleted” remains
- **Push token (only with your permission):** an identifier from Apple for your iPhone, so Barte can notify you when someone in your group adds, changes or deletes an expense, pays you back or writes in the chat. With it, Barte stores the app's language and your iPhone's region (e.g. “en-GB”), so notifications arrive in your language and number format
- **On your iPhone:** your first name as a suggestion for new groups (locally in the app settings) and your balance for the widget (locally in the storage shared by the app and the widget)

## Who sees the data

Only the members of a group can see it. You become a member by creating the group or opening an invite link or code. So treat invite links like a key: whoever has the link can join the group. People who are only entered as a name and don't use Barte see nothing.

Your payment details are only visible to people you share at least one group with. Nobody but you and the service that sends notifications sees push tokens.

Access is checked on the server.

## Processors

- **Nhost AB** (Sweden) runs the database, sign-in and server functions, hosted in Frankfurt (EU, AWS eu-central-1). [Nhost privacy policy](https://nhost.io/legal/privacy-policy)
- **Apple** provides “Sign in with Apple” and delivers notifications (Apple Push Notification service). [Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

The legal basis is the performance of the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): without this data, Barte can't share expenses between you. Payment details and notifications are optional and based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You delete payment details by clearing the fields; you turn off notifications in iOS Settings.

## Deleting

- **Expenses and people** can be deleted in the app.
- **Leave a group:** your account is disconnected from the group. Your name stays on the expenses so the others' accounts still add up.
- **Delete a group:** whoever created a group can delete it for everyone.
- **Delete your account** (About Barte → Delete Account): your account, payment details, chat messages and push tokens are deleted immediately, and Barte is disconnected from your Apple Account. Groups where nobody else uses Barte are deleted too. In shared groups, your name stays as a plain entry on the expenses and in the history.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object. Just send an email. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
